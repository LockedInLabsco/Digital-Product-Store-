# Not4Normal brand assets

Replace the temporary typographic wordmark in `src/components/BrandMark.tsx`
after the official files are supplied.

Expected production filenames:

- `not4normal-logo-black.svg`
- `not4normal-logo-white.svg`
- `not4normal-symbol-black.svg`
- `not4normal-symbol-white.svg`
- `not4normal-favicon.svg`
- `not4normal-social-preview.jpg`

Keep SVG artwork tightly cropped to its viewBox and preserve the original
aspect ratio. Do not convert official vector artwork to raster for navigation
or footer use.
